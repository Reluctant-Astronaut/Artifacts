ARTIFACTS

a Reluctant Astronaut plugin suite  ·  made with AI and GitHub

Four plugins to make music feel real, warm and full:

  • Console RA-1: a desk channel with transformer and glue compression.
  • Kintsugi: valve, tape and a slow, living instability.
  • Duality: two kinds of space, reverb and impulse response, and the point where they become one.
  • Anemoia: echoes and a cloud of memories.

The installer puts them in the standard place on your Mac, where every music program looks. You'll be asked for your Mac password.

Installs to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 and /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components. Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or later.
